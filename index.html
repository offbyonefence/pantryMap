Find Food Help

Enter your ZIP code or address to find food banks and pantries near you. Everyone welcome — no matter who you are or what you believe.

Private by design — no tracking, ever. ZIP search and location stay on your device. Typing a street address sends only that address to OpenStreetMap. More on privacy.

About & privacy

This site is a free, community-maintained directory of food banks and food pantries. Every listing serves anyone in need — religious affiliation of an organization is shown for information only, and no listing here may require religious participation to receive food.

Privacy: no analytics, no cookies, no ads, no tracking of any kind. ZIP code search uses a lookup table downloaded with the page, so your ZIP never leaves your browser. The "use my location" button uses your browser's own location feature; the result stays on your device. Only two optional actions contact an outside service, and both go to the nonprofit OpenStreetMap: searching by street address, and loading map images if you open the map.

Spot an error, or know a food bank we're missing? Suggest a change on GitHub — no coding needed, there's a simple form.
